**Define the Time and Place for Your Experiment**
<img src="/img/fern/when.png" alt="1"/>

In this step, you’ll set the **time** and **location** for your audience. These options allow you to control the temporal and geographic context of your experiment.

- When:
 Specify a particular year (e.g., **2024**) or choose a broader timeframe like "the early 80s." The system performs best when focusing on past or present time periods.

- Where:
 Select the **geographic location** by choosing a specific country for your audience.
