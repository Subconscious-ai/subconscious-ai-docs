## How to read the score

- **Spearman rho** ranks the paper's AMCEs and the synthetic AMCEs and asks how well the two orders agree. 1.0 is the paper itself. The interval is Fieller's on Fisher's z; when it covers zero the study is titled red.
- **The human ceiling is not 1.0.** Two random halves of the same human sample agree at about 0.91 to 0.96 where we have measured it (Hainmueller 2014 and Ballard-Rosa 2016). Normalized accuracy is rho divided by that ceiling; where no ceiling is measured, none is shown.
- **Noise floor.** Two same-design synthetic runs differ by about 0.025 in rho at 1,500 choices and 0.009 at 6,000. Differences smaller than that are not evidence.
- **Two intervals on every level.** The horizontal bar is the paper's own 95% interval; the vertical bar is the synthetic estimate's cluster-robust 95% interval from the same estimator. Where a paper published no error, no bar is drawn.

## The Golden-15, the benchmark of record

Chosen on the quality of the human study alone, since 2026-09-06: the source PDF is confirmed to be the paper, it is a real choice experiment, the paper's own analytic N is at least 500, at least ten published coefficients are numeric, and the paper states both the opt-out and the tasks per respondent. Qualifying studies are ranked by human N. No fidelity score, historic or current, enters the selection, so a good score on this set cannot come from picking the studies we already matched. The rule and the full ranked list live in the `Subconscious-ai/Ditto` repository (`docs/golden-15.md`, `data/golden15_candidates.csv`).
