# Dashboard

Once you're set up, take a moment to explore your dashboard. Here’s what you’ll find:

* **Join Our Discord**: Become part of our vibrant community, where you can ask questions, get support, and stay connected.
* **Experiment Showcase**: See how others are using Subconscious AI to run experiments and learn about our process for proving bioequivalence.
* **Run Your Own Experiment**: Set up your first causal experiment to uncover the "why" behind human behaviors, with step-by-step guidance to ensure success.
