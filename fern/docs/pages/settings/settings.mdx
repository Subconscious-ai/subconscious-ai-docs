## What is the Settings - Plan Subscription Page?
The **Settings - Plan Subscription Page** allows users to view and manage their subscription to **Subconscious.ai**. It provides details about the available plan, its features, and the pricing structure, making it easy for users to subscribe and unlock the full potential of the platform.

## What Does This Page Offer?

### 1. Subscription Details:
- Displays the current subscription plan.
- Shows a list of features included in the selected plan.

### 2. Plan Pricing and Options:
- Highlights the cost of the subscription (e.g., $1,000/month for the Standard Plan).
- Includes a **Subscribe** button for users who wish to activate or upgrade their plan.

### 3. Key Features of the Plan:

- **Unlimited Experiments:** Conduct as many experiments as needed without restrictions.
- **Predict Market Impacts:** Gain actionable insights into how products or services will perform in real-world markets.
- **Tailored Messaging:** Create unique and effective messages optimized for specific audiences or user groups.
- **Measure Market Metrics:** Analyze demand, market share, and price elasticity to refine product and marketing strategies.
