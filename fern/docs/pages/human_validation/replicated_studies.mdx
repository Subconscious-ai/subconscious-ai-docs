## What is the Experiments Showcase Page?

The **Experiments Showcase Page** is a curated library of real-world experiments that demonstrate Subconscious.ai’s capabilities in conducting high-quality, bioequivalent causal research. These experiments replicate findings from published academic studies, showcasing how Subconscious.ai can generate insights with the same rigor and reliability as traditional human-led studies.

## What does this page offer?

### 1. Experiment Repository
- A collection of pre-conducted experiments from various domains, such as healthcare, sustainability, and policy.
- Each experiment includes detailed references to its original study for transparency and academic alignment.

### 2. Search Functionality
- Use the search bar to find specific experiments by title, author, experiment ID, or journal.

### 3. Access to Key Details
For each experiment, users can view:
- Study title and authors.
- Publication details (journal name, volume, issue, and DOI).
- A brief summary of the experiment’s scope and findings.

### 4. Request Paper Replication
- If you don’t see a study you’re interested in, click the **"Request Paper Replication"** button to ask Subconscious.ai to replicate a specific experiment.

### 5. Bioequivalence Assurance
- The experiments showcased here demonstrate Subconscious.ai’s ability to replicate human decision-making accurately, with results comparable to the best-designed human-led studies.
