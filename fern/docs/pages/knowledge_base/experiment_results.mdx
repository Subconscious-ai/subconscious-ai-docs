## Your Insights Hub

The Experiment Results Page is where you’ll see all the answers you’ve been waiting for. Let’s break it down step by step:

### How to Know Your Results Are Ready

#### Time to Complete
Most experiments take **3 to 5 minutes**.

#### Notifications
- You’ll see a **red dot** in the notification tab when your results are ready.  
- If you’ve enabled email updates, you’ll get an email too.  

**Note:** Make sure to open the notification tab to see the results!  

### Accessing Your Results

1. Expand the **Experiment Results Card** in the dashboard.  
2. Click the Market Simulation Button for a detailed breakdown.

### The Summary Graph (AMCE Graph)  

<img src="/docs/images/amce.png" alt="1"/>

The **AMCE Graph** (Average Marginal Component Effect) is a powerful visualization used in conjoint analysis. It reveals how individual attributes or components of a product influence user preferences by calculating the average effect of each attribute while holding all others constant.  

#### Graph Details  
- **Attributes (Y-Axis):** Represents the different features of the product (e.g., "Free Shipping" or "Red Color").  
- **Marginal Effects (X-Axis):**  
  - Positive values show features that **increase** the likelihood of user preference.  
  - Negative values show features that **decrease** the likelihood.  
- **Confidence Intervals:** Horizontal lines indicate the level of certainty around each estimate.  

This graph is critical for understanding which features drive the biggest impact on decision-making and helps prioritize product improvements.
