## How to Pull/Download Graphs, Page Content, and Data Insights

### Download Graphs as PDFs
- Use the **Download Tab** near each graph.

### Download Raw Data (CSV)
- Located under the page title in the **Market Simulator**.

### Export Insights for Sharing
- Share experiment results by clicking the **Share Link** button on either the Result Card or Market Simulation page.

## Sharing Your Experiment Results

### How to Share
- Use the **Share Link** button on the Experiment Result Card or Market Simulation page.

### What Happens
- A shareable link is copied to your clipboard, which you can send to collaborators or stakeholders.
