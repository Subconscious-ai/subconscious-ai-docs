## Create Your Account and Set Up Your First Experiment

### Sign Up
- Create an account and log in to the platform.

### Explore the Dashboard
- Get familiar with your main control panel where all your experiments live.

### Start Your First Experiment
1. Click **"New Experiment."**
2. Define what you want to learn (e.g., what features make a product appealing?).
3. Set up your audience (e.g., demographics, location, or interests).

> **Tip:** Don’t worry if it feels overwhelming. Our AI walks you through every step with suggestions and guidance.
